ABOUT PHYSIOELITE

Physiotherapy with a movement-first approach.

Hi, I’m Bansi Kansara, a physiotherapist focused on helping people understand their body, manage pain and return to the activities that matter to them.

My approach combines clinical assessment, therapeutic exercise, rehabilitation and movement-based fitness so your care is not only about reducing symptoms, but also about building confidence and capacity.

Professional care, at your home.

If travelling to a clinic is difficult or you prefer one-to-one care in your own environment, home physiotherapy can make rehabilitation more convenient.

  • Exercise-based rehabilitation at home
  • Functional training around your daily routine
  • Progress tracking and home exercise guidance
